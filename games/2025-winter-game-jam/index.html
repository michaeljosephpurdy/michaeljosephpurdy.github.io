It's our year! Ange in!

It's up to you to get the trophy - COYS!

== CONTROLS ==
SPACEBAR: pause/unpause the game
LEFT_CLICK: place action
RIGHT_CLICK: remove action
LEFT_DRAG: move action
A: previous action
D: next action
R: restart level
M: toggle audio
