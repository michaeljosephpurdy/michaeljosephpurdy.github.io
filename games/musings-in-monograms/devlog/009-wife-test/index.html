009-wife-test

20261113 · original on itch.io

The ‘wife/spouse/partner test’ in game development is when you give your partner your game or prototype and have them play it without you guiding or helping them. My wife isn’t a ‘gamer’ by any means, but she does play games. She is active in Monopoly, Ticket to Ride, Clue, and other games on her iPad, playing with randos online.

If she can pick up a project I have made, and understand what to do within 5 minutes, then the project passes the test. If she has fun, then the project is probably pretty awesome.

I handed my wife this project and even after going through the tutorial, she didn’t really get it. I did the same thing to my son (13), and he also didn’t really get it.

So, the current tutorial has to be replaced with something that is:

  1. engaging
  2. informative
  3. brief

I am currently thinking that I need to prioritize those three ideas, in that order.

Also, something I have been worrying about with the collage-based level section, is that I have less of an opportunity to guide players through a ramp up of puzzles. I’ll have to think about that more in the future though. Maybe I try to group easy puzzles together and have the initial cursor position there or something.

Regardless, the current state of player onboarding is what has been holding me back from publishing the most recent build.

The problem is, is that I’ve hit a bit of writers block with the tutorial.

So, currently my plan is to play through some tutorials of popular nonogram games, like Walfie’s Nonograms.
