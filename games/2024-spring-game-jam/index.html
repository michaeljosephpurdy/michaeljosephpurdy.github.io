Lots of Sides

Lots of Sides is my entry to gamedev-interest 2024 spring game jam.  This is a Geometry Dash-like game.

Couldn't start the jam till the 5th, but had fun making this.  I planned on building level system but ran out of time :) so there is only Endless mode.

You'll have to hit fullscreen to be able to play the game, or download it.

Play with volume on :)

Theme was 'changing sides'.

Controls:

Left/Right arrow: change your sides

Down arrow: change your side

Up arrow: jump

Space: (menu) confirm

Return: (menu) go back
